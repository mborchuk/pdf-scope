# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Discuss the code, not the person. Assume the other side is trying to
help.

## Expected behaviour

- Keep discussion technical, specific and civil.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change; scope is a legitimate reason.
- Give people time to answer. This is a spare-time project.
- Respect privacy: never post confidential documents, personal data or
  credentials in issues, pull requests or attached files.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Discrimination or exclusionary jokes on any grounds.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, deliberate derailing, or repeated
  ignoring of maintainer decisions.
- Publishing someone's private information.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
other space where this project is being worked on.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues or pull requests, and
block accounts that behave unacceptably. Serious or repeated cases lead to a
permanent block.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the
repository's issue-reporting channels; describe what happened and link to the
relevant discussion. Reports are handled discreetly.

This document is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), condensed for a
project of this size.
